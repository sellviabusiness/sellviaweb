# SellVia Landing Page Design System

## Design Philosophy

The website should feel like a serious early-stage product, not a marketing campaign.

Avoid:
- Startup clichés
- Heavy gradients
- Floating glassmorphism cards
- Excessive shadows
- Glow effects
- Animated blobs
- AI-generated illustrations
- Generic SaaS screenshots
- Overly colorful interfaces
- Marketing buzzword sections

The design should communicate:

- Confidence
- Clarity
- Simplicity
- Trust
- Product-first thinking

Reference feeling:
- HackerRank
- Google Bug Hunters
- Stripe Docs
- Linear
- Vercel
- Measurement Plan
- Layer9

The page should feel designed by humans, not generated by an AI landing page builder.

---

# Typography

## Primary Font

Outfit

Used for:
- Headlines
- Hero copy
- Navigation
- CTA buttons
- Section titles

Characteristics:
- Clean
- Modern
- Slightly geometric
- Strong visual presence

---

## Secondary Font

Figtree

Used for:
- Paragraphs
- Supporting text
- Labels
- Form fields
- Cards
- Metadata

Characteristics:
- Neutral
- Highly readable
- Less personality than Outfit
- Allows headlines to stand out



---

# Layout Principles

## Container

Max width:

1280px

Content width:

1200px

Readable text width:

640px–720px

---

## Spacing

Large breathing room.

Section padding:

120px top
120px bottom

Hero:

160px top
120px bottom

Avoid cramped layouts.

Whitespace is a design element.

---

# Visual Language

Everything should feel:

- Sharp
- Structured
- Intentional

Use:

- Thin borders
- Grid layouts
- Large typography
- Strong spacing

Avoid:

- Decorative elements
- Random shapes
- Abstract AI graphics
- Excessive icons

---

# Shadows

Avoid shadows completely.

Allowed:

Very subtle shadow only when necessary.

Example:

box-shadow:
0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)

Nothing beyond that.

No glow effects.

No blurred shadows.

No elevation system.

Use borders instead.

---

# Border Radius

Small.

Buttons:
10px

Cards:
12px

Inputs:
10px

Never exceed:

16px

Avoid pill-heavy designs.

---

# Hero Section

## Structure

Centered layout.

Small announcement badge.

Large headline.

Supporting paragraph.

Email waitlist form.

Optional product visual.

---

## Headline Style

Maximum:
2–3 lines

Large.

Bold.

Confident.

Examples:

Turn Creators Into Your Sales Team.

Affiliate Marketing Without The Complexity.

Where Businesses And Creators Grow Together.

---

## Hero Width

Headline:
max-width 900px

Description:
max-width 600px

---

# Navigation

Minimal.

Logo left.

Links center/right.

Single CTA button.

Links:

How It Works

For Businesses

For Creators

Join Waitlist

Avoid dropdowns.

Avoid mega menus.

---

# Cards

Cards should feel like content blocks.

Not marketing components.

Structure:

Title

Description

Optional number

Optional border

Avoid:

Icons above every heading.

Huge illustrations.

Over-designed layouts.

---

# Section Design

Every section should have a purpose.

No filler sections.

No "trusted by 1000+ companies" unless real.

No fake testimonials.

No fake metrics.

---

# Animations

Very restrained.

Allowed:

- Fade in
- Slight opacity transitions
- Border color transitions
- Subtle movement (2–4px)

Avoid:

- Parallax
- Floating effects
- Scroll hijacking
- Mouse-follow animations
- Infinite motion

Animations should never become the focus.

---

# Forms

Simple.

One goal:

Collect emails.

Fields:

Email

Optional role selector:

Business
Creator

Button:

Join Waitlist

No long forms.

No unnecessary questions.

---

# Imagery

Use only:

- Product wireframes
- Abstract grid systems
- Structured diagrams
- Minimal platform mockups

Avoid:

- Stock photos
- AI illustrations
- 3D SaaS renders
- Random people images
- Corporate office imagery

---

# Grid System

Desktop:

12-column grid

Tablet:

8-column grid

Mobile:

4-column grid

Everything should align to the grid.

---

# CTA Style

Primary CTA:

Solid background

White text

Clear border

No gradients.

No glow.

No flashy hover states.

Hover:

Slight opacity reduction.

Border change.

Nothing more.

---

# Overall Feeling

If someone lands on the website, they should think:

"This looks like a real product being built by people who understand the problem."

Not:

"This looks like another AI-generated SaaS landing page."

The design should prioritize clarity over excitement, trust over hype, and structure over decoration.


# Color System

## Primary Background

#000000

Use for:
- Entire page background
- Hero section
- Navigation
- Footer

The website should feel dark, clean, and premium.

---

## Primary Accent

#BFFF13

Use sparingly.

Allowed:

- Primary CTA
- Small highlights
- Active states
- Form focus states
- Tiny decorative accents

Avoid:

- Large color blocks
- Full-width green sections
- Green gradients
- Green backgrounds behind content

The green should feel earned.

If everything is green, nothing stands out.
